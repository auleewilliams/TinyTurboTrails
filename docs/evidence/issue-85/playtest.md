# Sunset Site readability playtest — pending

This is a session checklist, not a completed result. Issue #85 requires a
firsthand six-year-old playtest; screenshots and automated play do not satisfy it.

Record the tested commit/build, date, browser, viewport/display scale, input
device and observer. Record the player's age without their name or other
identifying information. Use a fresh run at normal gameplay size and avoid
explaining each sprite's role before the first encounter.

1. Open `/?scene=adventure`, select **SUNSET SITE** with Left/Right, then Space
   (or a controller face button). Visit the gate and girder deck.
2. At the cone/mixer cluster, ask “What do you think happens if Henry touches
   that?” Record the answer and first action. After contact, record whether
   the player understands the damage feedback. Recognizing “avoid” alone does
   not establish that the player expects harm.
3. At a pneumatic jack, ask “What do you think this does?” Record whether the
   player predicts a bounce, approaches it and understands the launch.
4. At scaffold, pipes and scrub, ask “What can Henry safely go past here?”
   Record any scenery avoided because it looks harmful, including location.
5. At a cement slime, record whether the player recognizes a moving enemy.
   Allow another encounter after feedback; distinguish learning from the
   player's initial interpretation.
