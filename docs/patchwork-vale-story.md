# Henry Turbo and Patchwork Vale

> Henry Turbo loves finding out what's around the next bend. With his backpack
> ready, he sets off to explore Patchwork Vale—from grassy hills and tall
> treehouses to snowy peaks and the seaside. Every trail has something to
> discover, and Henry chooses where to go next.

Henry Turbo is an explorer. Curiosity is his reason to visit each place.
Patchwork Vale is an original country of chunky hills, crooked bridges, small
settlements and wild places. Its paths are the Tiny Trails: Plains, Quarry Run,
Treetop Timbers, Sunset Site, Frost Ridge and Sandy Cove. A sandwich sticking
out of his little pack adds gentle humour.

There is no crisis, gathering, deadline or overarching mission. Each trail is
an independent outing: choose a place, explore, discover collectibles and
celebrate reaching the end. Letters and word stickers are optional discoveries;
they never gate a trail or its completion. No overall six-trail ending,
completion obligation, quest, repair, construction or interior is promised.
Construction, digging and enterable sub-areas remain separate future discussions
(#186 and #192). This premise was agreed with the owner in #195 and supersedes
the earlier storm-and-gathering opening from #120.

## Starting and finishing an outing

Both `/` and `/?scene=adventure` start, and reload, directly on the trail map
with Plains selected. All six destinations are open from the start. The map
shows the six illustrated landmarks, decorative bunting, the Patchwork Vale name
and a short description of each place; Plains reads **Over grassy hills**.

Every trail, Plains included, finishes the same way: its own landmark, Henry's
cheer, the **Another trail explored!** message, gem/star results and Replay /
Next trail / Choose trail. There is no picture story, replay control, reunion
picture or story-only key or controller binding.

## Celebration integration

The result panel pairs the trail's landmark with Henry's eight-pose jump and
cheer, gem/star totals, and immediate navigation actions. The presentation timer
resets on every finish and run reset, plays the 150ms poses once over 1.2
seconds, and holds victory. A bounded sparkle burst starts near the peak and
ends by 1.8 seconds. Reduced motion immediately uses victory and static
sparkles. Pause/focus loss freezes updates. No celebration state is passed to
deterministic gameplay simulation or browser storage.

## Owner-led follow-up (not yet performed)

With sound muted, let Henry choose an outing before explaining anything.

- Ask him to pick a place from the map pictures and say where he is going.
- At the finish, ask what he did there. Does he understand completion as having explored that place?
- Ask him to choose another trail from the map, then return using Choose trail.
- Ask him to point out a gold star versus an ordinary gem and explain what he can collect.
- Watch whether he recognises crouch, jump and cheer and can still find Replay, Next trail and Choose trail.
- Try controller input and the small window. Record any adult help or coaching needed.
