# Henry Turbo and Patchwork Vale

Henry Turbo is an eager little trail scout. He thinks every trail leads to
someone worth meeting, and he wants to see what is over the next hill.
Patchwork Vale is an original country of chunky hills, crooked bridges,
small settlements and wild places. Its community paths are the Tiny Trails.

The Great Rumble was a powerful storm that damaged old routes and separated
neighbours. The annual Vale Get-Together is coming. Henry wants everyone to
make it there. Keep this friendly and reassuring: nobody is in frightening
peril. A sandwich sticking out of his little pack adds gentle humour.

The broader direction is reopening routes by exploring, helping neighbours,
delivering supplies or finding bridge parts. **Only exploration is implemented
here.** No repairs, delivery, inventory, vehicle, dialogue or quest system is
implied by a completed trail. Plains ends with reaching a friend by taking the
hill path around a broken bridge. It does not claim the bridge was repaired.

## Visual storyboard

| Picture | Meaning without words | Shared visual anchors |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening 1 | Henry and a neighbour prepare a happy picnic | Red/yellow/blue bunting, table, sandwich |
| Opening 2 | Gentle rain has broken the bridge; friends are separated | Same purple-coated neighbour, two safe banks, alternative hill trail |
| Opening 3 / Plains goal | Walk over the hill to reach the waving neighbour | Henry's hat/vest/boots, sandwich pack, trail arch and bunting |
| Plains payoff | Henry reached his friend; they greet each other | High five, same neighbour, arch, picnic and bunting |

Pictures carry the meaning. Short captions are separate canvas text, with
picture descriptions on the canvas accessible label. No narration is required.
The three opening pages wait for the player. Large previous, next and skip
symbols have accessible button names. Keyboard arrows/Space and controller
D-pad/face buttons choose and activate them; pointer and Tab/Enter also work.
Skipping or finishing returns to unrestricted trail selection. The opening
does not reappear on retries, Replay, Next trail or Choose trail. Reload starts
a new memory-only session. The map's rewind symbol reopens the story; R or the
standard controller View button (8) does the same. The Plains result picture
can be enlarged and returned from using the same inputs.

The map uses all six existing illustrated landmarks and adds gathering bunting,
the Patchwork Vale name, and short trail context. Plains previews its visual
destination; an early-run picture cue leaves the movement line and HUD clear.
Other trails remain fully open and keep their existing mechanics and soundtracks.

## Celebration integration

The result panel pairs the reunion (or other trail's landmark) with Henry's
eight-pose jump and cheer, gem/star totals, and immediate navigation actions.
The presentation timer resets on every finish and run reset, plays the 150ms
poses once over 1.2 seconds, and holds victory. A bounded sparkle burst starts
near the peak and ends by 1.8 seconds. Reduced motion immediately uses victory
and static sparkles. Pause/focus loss freezes updates; the enlarged picture
also suspends the underlying finish timer. No story or celebration state is
passed to deterministic gameplay simulation or browser storage.

## Parent-led follow-up (not yet performed)

With sound muted, let Henry explore before explaining the story. Hide captions
or ask him to look just at the pictures; do not read them aloud first.

- Ask what happened at the bridge and who Henry wants to reach. Record his own words.
- Ask him to select Plains and one other trail from their pictures, then return.
- Ask him to point out a gold star versus an ordinary gem and explain what he can collect.
- At the end, ask what changed for the neighbour. Does he infer reaching a friend, rather than repairing a bridge?
- Watch whether he recognises crouch, jump and cheer and can still find Replay, Next trail and Choose trail.
- Try the previous/next/skip/replay symbols, controller input, and the small window. Record any adult help needed.

Record device, viewport, input, exact observations and resulting adjustments
in the milestone evidence. Automated tests and adult inspection cannot establish
Henry's understanding, enjoyment or independent use. Real controller hardware,
Safari on macOS, and the supported installed browser-version matrix remain
human checks beyond bundled Playwright engines.
